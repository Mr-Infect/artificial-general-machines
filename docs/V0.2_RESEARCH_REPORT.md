# AGM v0.2 Research Report

**Question.** Is there measurable evidence that AGM is learning transferable principles of computational organization, rather than merely searching, mutating, or memorizing architectures?

**Answer: weak and direction-dependent; not a general capability.** By the letter of the pre-registered criteria (which required a significant cost reduction in *any* source→target cell) all 7 of 7 are met. Substantively, transferred memory reduced adaptation cost in 1 of 6 directions (C->B), *increased* it significantly in 4 (B->A, C->A, A->C, B->C) and had no effect in 1. The pre-registered criterion was too lenient; it is kept here unchanged and tightened in v0.3 (majority of transfer environments must improve and none may be significantly harmed).

Evidence base: 330 finished experiments, executor(s) ['process'], code at commit(s) `47c6a8b2eb86`, `cad9294d4e82`, `d07b0f410b21` (report generated at `47c6a8b2eb86`). Pre-registered criteria met: **7 of 7**.

## 1. Hypothesis and pre-registered success criteria

AGM hypothesis (v0.2): a machine can learn generalizable principles of computational organization that let it construct, evaluate, modify and reuse task-specific architectures under the constraints of an unfamiliar environment while accounting for the cost of adaptation. ## 2. Method summary

- Three synthetic domains: A network anomaly, B scientific verification, C resource-constrained planning (structurally different vocabulary and winners; `docs/domain_planning.md`).
- Identical data, seeds, environment schedule, resource limits, sandbox runner and scoring for every compared agent. Comparisons are paired by seed.
- Target quality per dataset = validation quality of the best-decile point of the enumerated wide architecture space (lab oracle; recorded in provenance). "Evaluations-to-target" = cumulative candidate evaluations when an agent first reached it.
- Memory is per seed; warm conditions read memory written by exploration-mode source runs in the *other* domains. Principles are extracted by deterministic rules, tested only under conditions that did not produce them, and counted by distinct experiments.
- Statistics: mean, median, sd, bootstrap 95% CI, paired delta with CI, Cohen's d on paired differences, sign counts, failure rate, adaptation overhead. Nothing is collapsed into one score.

## 9. Discrimination among the candidate causes of the v0.1 negative result

1. **Insufficient structural memory** — v0.2 memories hold 960 files with 2735 principle records ({'INCONCLUSIVE': 526, 'PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED': 36, 'UNTESTED': 2173}) plus architecture patterns; retrieval, instantiation and reuse happened in every cell (criterion C). Memory *quantity* is no longer the limiting factor.
2. **Insufficiently different environments** — Domain C has a different component vocabulary, scoring and winners; transfer into and out of it behaved like A↔B (['C->A', 'C->B', 'A->C', 'B->C']). Domain similarity does not explain the result.
3. **Insufficient architecture search space** — the space was widened to ~255 registry points per domain; the evaluation budget covers only a fraction, so candidate ordering by prior knowledge *could* have mattered. It reduced evaluations in C->B.
4. **Weak architecture representation** — distance analysis (§7): the fraction of runs whose final architecture is structurally identical to the initial one ranges 0.17–1.00 across conditions. The IR expresses the space; whether its useful region is reachable without memory is what §4 measures.
5. **Lack of principle abstraction** — principles were abstracted (roles, conditions, effects) and tested; the test outcomes were predominantly contradicted ({'contradicted': 181, 'inconclusive': 27, 'supported': 25}). The abstraction exists; its predictions were mostly wrong under new conditions.
6. **Adaptation economics behaving incorrectly** — criterion E holds; decision and cost-estimate errors are reported in §6. Economics is not what suppressed transfer: the warm agent evaluated *more*, not fewer, candidates.
7. **The hypothesis itself** — with A–C met and D/F/G met, the remaining explanation consistent with all measurements is that, in these domains, the architectural experience the machine can currently extract does not encode anything that shortens adaptation. Whether this is a property of the rule-based extractor, of the synthetic domains, or of the hypothesis cannot be separated by v0.2 alone; the LLM proposer (Experiment 9) was not run.

## 10. Alternative explanations and limitations

- The domains are synthetic and their useful architectures are cheap; a cheapest-first cold search reaches the top-decile target after on average {'B->A': 1.5, 'C->A': 1.5, 'A->B': 4.77, 'C->B': 4.77, 'A->C': 3.48, 'B->C': 3.48} evaluations. Where that number is close to 1 there is little room for any prior to help (a ceiling effect of the benchmark, not evidence for the hypothesis).
- Principle extraction is rule-based with six templates; richer extraction might produce different principles. Tests use the validation slice, which for some datasets has few positives (quantized quality).
- Registry "models" are simulated cost profiles; the 30-seed program used the process executor (identical for all agents); Docker was used for the v0.1 freeze and the example manifests.
- Experiment 9 (LLM proposer) was implemented as a validated pipeline but not executed: no API key on the host and, by design, it comes last.

## 11. Conclusion

**Answer: weak and direction-dependent; not a general capability.** By the letter of the pre-registered criteria (which required a significant cost reduction in *any* source→target cell) all 7 of 7 are met. Substantively, transferred memory reduced adaptation cost in 1 of 6 directions (C->B), *increased* it significantly in 4 (B->A, C->A, A->C, B->C) and had no effect in 1. The pre-registered criterion was too lenient; it is kept here unchanged and tightened in v0.3 (majority of transfer environments must improve and none may be significantly harmed).

Direction-level summary of the primary metric (paired Δ adaptation cost, memory − cold): reduced in ['C->B']; increased in ['B->A', 'C->A', 'A->C', 'B->C']; no significant change in ['A->B'].

What the laboratory established in v0.2: (i) architectural principles can be extracted, stored inspectably, instantiated in a different domain's vocabulary and tested with recorded epistemic outcomes; (ii) the machine records and can be audited on its adaptation economics; (iii) over 30 seeds and a 3×3 transfer matrix, prior architectural knowledge did not reduce the cost of finding a useful architecture in a new domain. The most valuable output of v0.2 is that this negative result is now measured with confidence intervals rather than suspected.
