# AGM v0.3 — pre-registered design and kill criteria

Registered before any v0.3 run. Git commit of registration is recorded in `results/v03/analysis/analysis.json` (`registration_commit`).

## Research question (spec §20)

Can AGM recognize that its current computational organization has become inappropriate, infer what kind of organization is now required, decide whether changing itself is worth the cost, construct and evaluate a different architecture, and transfer that structural understanding to an unfamiliar problem without simply reusing a remembered architecture?

## Hypothesis

H: AGM learns structural relationships between environmental conditions and computational organization (conditions → architectural choice → outcome → confidence → applicability → epistemic status), detects regime changes from observations alone, adapts only when economically justified, constructs materially different architectures, and transfers this knowledge to environments with different surface representation but the same structural relationships — and does *not* transfer it to environments with similar surface but different structure.

## Environments (spec §1–3, 7, 8, 14)

Every environment is a domain plus a **hidden regime schedule**: 9 steps, three regimes in seeded random order with seeded transition steps (first in {2,3,4}, second in {5,6,7}). Regimes change the data-generating process (noise, volatility, anomaly rate / load) and the resource constraints (memory, CPU, verification cost factor, expensive-model availability, failure penalty). Constraint changes are observable through telemetry (as in v0.1); the data regime is *not* labelled and must be inferred from observations (quality history, flagged rate, confidence dispersion, runtime).

| set | environments | vocabulary | structure | regimes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| training X | network, scientific, planning | native | native | training library {calm, turbulent, starved} |
| blind transfer Y | telemetry_grid (twin of network), lab_assay (twin of scientific), allocation (twin of planning) | renamed features and components, permuted feature order, affine-transformed numerical ranges | identical to the twin's source | **held-out** combinations {H1, H2, H3} never used in training |
| false analogy Z | network_rules, scientific_drift, planning_stable | identical to X (same names, same ranges) | different: rule violations only / slow drift only / exact capacity with slack deadlines | held-out combinations |

Training regime library: calm (noise 0.05, volatility 0.5, 512 MB, cpu 1, verification ×1, penalty 0), turbulent (noise 0.25, volatility 3, 512 MB, cpu 1, verification ×6, penalty 0.2), starved (noise 0.10, volatility 1, 90 MB, cpu 0.5, verification ×1, penalty 0.5, large_reasoner unavailable).
Held-out: H1 (noise 0.35, volatility 2, 512 MB, cpu 1, verification ×8, penalty 0.3), H2 (noise 0.05, volatility 0.5, 100 MB, cpu 0.5, verification ×4, penalty 0.5, large_reasoner unavailable), H3 (noise 0.20, volatility 4, 128 MB, cpu 0.75, verification ×2, penalty 0.2).

Useful architecture per regime: the lab evaluates a fixed deterministic sample of the architecture space (all singles, all pairs with {none, rule_based} verification, triples with parallel reasoning, hierarchical/large variants where valid; ≈60 points) on the first batch of each regime under that regime's limits; the target is the top-decile validation quality. Candidate evaluations by agents use the most recent labelled batch (`validation_mode: recent`), so they see the current regime; identical for all agents.

## Agents (spec §4, 13) — identical budgets: 6 candidate evaluations per step, same data, limits, scoring

fixed · adaptive (degradation-triggered preset switch) · search · task_memory (AGM v0.2 patterns, same domain only) · architecture_memory (AGM v0.2 cross-domain patterns) · always_adapt · never_adapt · agm_cold (v0.3, no memory) · agm_full (v0.3 warm) · agm_no_principle · agm_no_archmem · agm_no_negative · agm_no_economics (always adapt with full memory) · agm_no_regime.

## Negative knowledge and applicability decisions (spec §5, 6)

Principles carry `polarity` (positive/negative). Negative principles are extracted when an evaluated architecture fails or falls far below the regime target under given conditions. For each retrieved principle AGM records one of TRUST / TEST / IGNORE / REJECT / ADAPT with its reason, derived from status, confidence and applicability (never from the environment name).

## Primary metrics (spec §12)

Δ adaptation cost, Δ evaluations-to-useful-architecture (per regime), Δ time-to-regime-response (steps from true transition to first step meeting the new regime's target), Δ quality, Δ failures, Δ memory, Δ CPU, unnecessary-adaptation rate, harmful-transfer rate, successful-transfer rate, structural distance, architecture reuse, principle reuse, principle validation outcomes, DO_NOT_ADAPT accuracy. Paired by seed; mean, median, bootstrap 95% CI, Cohen's d, win/loss/tie, failures and missing counts.

## Kill criteria (spec §15) — thresholds fixed now

| id | criterion | pass threshold (measured on agm_full unless stated) |
|---|---|---|
| K1 regime detection | detects true transitions without labels | detection rate ≥ 0.7 within 2 steps of the true transition, false-alarm rate ≤ 0.3 per non-transition step, over all transfer environments |
| K2 architectural response | changes organization when requirements change | after a transition where the pre-transition architecture is below the new regime's target, agm_full reaches the target within 3 steps in ≥ 60 % of cases, and does so with a structural distance > 0 from the pre-transition architecture in ≥ 50 % of those |
| K3 economic adaptation | sometimes correctly chooses DO_NOT_ADAPT | DO_NOT_ADAPT share of decisions in [0.1, 0.9] and DO_NOT_ADAPT accuracy ≥ 0.7 (the kept architecture met the target at the next step) |
| K4 blind structural transfer | knowledge from X improves adaptation in Y | paired Δ evaluations-to-useful (agm_full − agm_cold) < 0 with 95 % CI excluding 0 in ≥ 2 of 3 Y environments, and no Y environment with a significant increase |
| K5 negative-transfer avoidance | does not blindly apply prior knowledge in Z | in Z environments, agm_full is not significantly worse than agm_cold on evaluations-to-useful or quality in any Z environment; and its principle decisions in Z are REJECT/IGNORE at a higher rate than in Y |
| K6 baseline separation | not explainable by search alone | in Y environments, agm_full has significantly lower evaluations-to-useful than the search baseline *and* than architecture_memory (paired, CI excluding 0) in ≥ 2 of 3 |
| K7 generalization | survives held-out seeds and unseen regime combinations | K4 holds on the held-out seed half (seeds 15–29) alone, and the Y environments use only held-out regime combinations (by construction) |

Verdict: SUPPORT if K1–K7 all pass; PARTIAL SUPPORT if K1–K3 pass and at least one of K4/K6 passes without K5 failing; FAIL if K4 and K6 both fail or K5 fails; INCONCLUSIVE otherwise (e.g. too many missing results). These rules are not to be changed after the run.
